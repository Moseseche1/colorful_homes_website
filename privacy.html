Privacy notice

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This notice explains how Colorful Homes Construction Company handles information submitted through the project enquiry form on this website.

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, organisation, requested service and the project details you choose to provide.

How we use it

We use this information to understand your enquiry, contact you about the project, prepare a response and maintain relevant business correspondence. If the website's HubSpot integration is enabled, the contact details are stored in our HubSpot CRM and the project enquiry is attached as a note to the contact record.

WhatsApp

After submitting the form, the website may open WhatsApp with a prepared message. You choose whether to send it. WhatsApp is operated by a third party and its own privacy terms apply.

Sharing and service providers

We do not offer enquiry details for sale. Website hosting, CRM and messaging providers may process information on our behalf to provide their services, subject to their own terms and applicable safeguards.

Retention and your choices

We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the business relationship and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion, subject to legal requirements.

Contact

For questions or requests about your information, contact colorfulhomes1@gmail.com or call +234 706 176 7623.

This notice is an operational website notice, not legal advice. Have it reviewed against the company's actual data handling, retention practices and applicable Nigerian privacy requirements before launch.
